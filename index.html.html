01 — About

A developer who builds
for the inbox.

I'm a BTech Computer Science graduate currently working as a Web Developer Trainee, where I build HTML/CSS email templates for marketing campaigns — a corner of web development most people overlook, and one that comes with its own hard constraints: every layout has to survive a dozen different email clients, each with their own rendering quirks.

My day-to-day involves translating design mockups into clean, table-based HTML and CSS, adding responsive layouts, embedding images, and wiring up links and CTAs for campaigns that need to just work, everywhere.

Alongside that, I have a foundation in C and C++ from my degree, and I'm actively growing into broader frontend and web development work.
